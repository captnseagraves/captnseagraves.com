# Case Study: NiftyApes

**Role:** Co-Founder, CEO & CTO
**Dates:** Oct 2021 – Aug 2024
**Team:** 10 people (engineering, product, operations)

## Hero Metrics
- **$4.2M raised** from Coinbase Ventures, Variant Fund, Fintech Collective, Robot Ventures, Polygon Ventures, The LAO, Flamingo DAO, and angels including David Hoffman, Ryan Sean Adams, Ben Lakoff
- **Two protocols shipped to Ethereum mainnet**, each with independent security audits
- **Invented the Harberger Style Lending Auction (HSLA)** — a novel on-chain mechanism for continuous price discovery in lending markets

## The Problem
NFT markets had tons of illiquid capital locked in JPEGs. Lenders wanted to underwrite against NFTs, and borrowers wanted liquidity without selling. Existing lending protocols priced loans badly, had slow liquidation mechanics, and couldn't support secondary debt markets.

## What I Built

### The HSLA Protocol (NFT Mortgage + HELOC)
A novel lending auction mechanism inspired by Harberger taxation. Instead of fixed-rate loans, rates continuously re-priced based on market competition. Anyone could refinance an existing loan at a better rate, triggering a bid war that converged to the market price in real time.

[Whitepaper →](https://whitepaper.niftyapes.money/)

### The Buy-Now-Pay-Later Protocol
A seller-financing mechanism for NFT sales, letting buyers pay over time and sellers receive principal + interest as a built-in DeFi primitive.

### What I Did Personally
- **Raised $4.2M in seed funding** — led the pitch, closed top-tier crypto VCs, ran the data room
- **Architected and authored the Solidity protocols** from scratch
- **Managed three independent security audits** across both protocols
- **Led a team of 10** across engineering, product, and operations; hired, mentored, and eventually laid off the team
- **Full P&L ownership** — burn rate, payroll, vendor contracts, investor reporting
- **Led go-to-market** — onboarded lenders, borrowers, buyers, sellers; ran community and social

## Impact
- Two novel protocols live on Ethereum mainnet
- Contributed new primitives to the DeFi lending space
- HSLA mechanism was cited as prior art in subsequent lending protocol designs

## The Wind-Down
When the NFT market collapsed in 2023-24, the economic thesis for NFT-collateralized lending collapsed with it. I made the call to wind down operations and return approximately one-third of invested capital to investors — an orderly shutdown including staff layoffs, vendor terminations, and honest conversations with our cap table.

This was the hardest professional decision of my career. It was also the one my investors respected the most. Several have offered to back me again.

## What I Learned
- **Raising capital is a product.** The pitch, the deck, the data room — they're UX for investors.
- **Team building is the real job of a founder.** You spend more time hiring and managing than coding.
- **Knowing when to stop is a skill.** Grinding through market conditions that invalidate your thesis isn't resilience — it's denial.
- **Investor references travel.** How you handle the downside determines whether you get a second chance.

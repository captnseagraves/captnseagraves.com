# Case Study: Dinner

**Role:** Founder
**Dates:** Mar 2025 – Jan 2026
**Product:** letsdinner.co — curated community dinner platform

## Hero Metric
**Full-stack consumer product across iOS, Android, and web**, shipped solo, with a multi-market paid-acquisition funnel achieving measurable cost-per-acquisition economics.

## The Problem
Adults in new cities struggle to build close friendships. Traditional social apps optimize for matches, not depth. I wanted to build a platform that brought curated groups of strangers together for dinners — high-context, intentional, offline.

## What I Built — End to End, Solo
- **React Native app** deployed to iOS and Android
- **Full-stack web application** — user management, in-app messaging, event orchestration
- **Marketing site** (letsdinner.co)
- **Multi-market paid-acquisition funnel** — tested across multiple cities with measurable ROAS
- **Branding, visual identity, and social presence**
- **Community operations playbook** — curation, logistics for real-world events

## Outcome
- Functional product shipped across 3 platforms (iOS, Android, web)
- Paid funnel achieved measurable cost-per-acquisition (~$0.50 return on every $1 spent in early tests)
- Multi-market launch executed

Ultimately, the business was a structural mismatch with my life — I started building Dinner while living in Brooklyn, New York, but transitioned to spending much of my time in Costa Rica. By that point the market felt very distant to serve. The model required high on-the-ground presence (content creation, photography, community curation), and I couldn't deliver that remotely eeffectively.

## What I Learned
- **Building across 3 platforms solo is a taste exercise.** You have to cut relentlessly. Every feature has to earn its place across iOS, Android, web.
- **Consumer acquisition is brutal.** Getting $0.50 back on $1 is actually pretty good for a cold-start community product — but "pretty good" doesn't pay the bills.
- **The business model has to fit your life, not just the market.** I built something that needed me to be in five places at once. That was a product-life fit problem, not a product-market fit problem.
- **Shipping teaches you things nothing else will.** I now know how to scope, build, and launch a cross-platform consumer product as a solo founder. That's permanent.

## Technical Depth (for the curious)
- **Backend:** Full-stack web app with user management, in-app messaging, event orchestration
- **Mobile:** React Native — deployed to iOS and Android
- **Infrastructure:** Deployed, monitored, and operated solo
- **Acquisition:** Paid funnel across Meta, Instagram, with multi-touch attribution
